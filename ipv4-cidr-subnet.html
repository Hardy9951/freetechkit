What does an IPv4 CIDR prefix mean?

Learn how to read an IPv4 CIDR prefix and calculate a subnet range using a worked /26 example.

Open the IPv4 subnet calculator

Read the prefix

An IPv4 address contains 32 bits. A /26 prefix fixes the first 26 bits as the network part and leaves 6 bits for addresses within the subnet. That gives 2 to the power of 6, or 64 total addresses.

Work through an example

Enter 192.0.2.70 and choose /26. The subnet mask is 255.255.255.192. The address falls in the 192.0.2.64–192.0.2.127 range. In a conventional subnet, .64 is the network address, .127 is the broadcast address, and .65 through .126 are the 62 usable host addresses.

Check edge cases

A /32 identifies one address. A /31 is commonly used for point-to-point links, where both addresses can be assigned to endpoints. Check the conventions of your equipment when planning an address range.
