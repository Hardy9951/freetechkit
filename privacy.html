Privacy notice

Browser tools

The tools process the values and files you enter in your browser. The toolbox loads its QR library from cdnjs, so your browser connects to that service when it loads the toolbox.

Hosting and external services

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub and cdnjs receive normal requests from your browser, which can include your IP address. Feedback and sponsor inquiries through GitHub Issues are public. Keep private information out of those posts.
